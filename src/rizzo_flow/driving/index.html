HOW IT WORKS

A decision engine behind the wheel.

CLM sees a structured snapshot of the road and chooses a target speed, a lane action, and an attention category. The browser converts those decisions into acceleration, braking, and steering.

Weather changes grip, braking distance, and visibility. The model sees nearby vehicles and pedestrians; traffic lights and road geometry are supplied as map information. Night reduces visibility further.

The optional emergency brake is deterministic and its interventions are counted separately. If inference becomes stale, the car brakes. API failures pause the drive. No scripted autopilot replaces CLM.

With a destination or a requested turn, a navigator supplies the route; CLM chooses pace, lane changes, and attention. The navigator recalculates after missed turns and stops before the destination junction. During free exploration CLM chooses the outgoing street. It can pass slower traffic and return right once clear. The map traces your actual route. Other traffic follows scripted rules; this is not a real-world autonomy benchmark. Decisions run locally. Simulation time pauses while the model is thinking.
